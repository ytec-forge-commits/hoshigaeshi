# Star Current — soft flute credits arrangement

[日本語](CREDITS.md)

An ending and credits arrangement of **Star Current / 星流**, composed for STAR REBOUND. The owner selected full-length audition 04 on 2026-11-04. A procedural flute melody with breath and gentle vibrato accompanies harp-like plucks, pad, bass and quiet response bells.

`credits-flute-v1.mp3` is byte-identical to the selected 39.285714-second audition. OGG was encoded from the same PCM24 WAV with libvorbis quality 6. No remix, loudness normalization or trimming was applied. The complete piece repeats with its closing decay intact. The first-stage music remains a separate asset.

Original composition, synthesis and arrangement retain the project's existing CC0 1.0 terms to the extent the provider may grant those rights. No exclusive rights in AI-assisted output are asserted.

The accompaniment's response bells use the CC0 1.0 sample `glock_medium_G4.wav` from **VSCO 2 Community Edition / Versilian Studios and contributors**, with pitch, filtering, envelope and room processing. The melody is synthesized. See the [upstream repository](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), [included CC0 text](VSCO-2-CE-CC0-LICENSE.txt) and [source, processing and SHA-256 ledger](PROVENANCE.json). No new external audio was downloaded for this adoption.
